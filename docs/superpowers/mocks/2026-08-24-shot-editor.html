Decisions baked into this mock
  • The screenshot keeps the site's own look (fonts, red rule, layout); only the words change. That's the point of "translate the page, then shoot".
  • Translated and Original are captured together; Bilingual is one click plus a ~1 s re-shoot from the source tab. Editing any translation uses the same re-shoot.
  • Card frame: warm Daylight gradient, 44–48 px padding, rounded shot, raised shadow, small badge. Plain = the bare capture for people who want a clean file.
  • The text panel is the seed for step 2: every row is a real block with its original, so word cards and Paraphrase attach here later.
  • Export size (native / 2× / 1× / ½) and format (PNG / JPEG) live in step 1. Books (collect shots into named books, one shot in several books, export a book as PDF) is step 4 in the spec's roadmap.
