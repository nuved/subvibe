Watch any video in your language. SubVibe translates a video's subtitles with
AI while you watch — perfectly timed to the player, with a karaoke highlight
that lights up each word as it's spoken, and optional AI voices that read the
translation aloud.

Bring your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google). Translations go
directly from your browser to the provider you picked — no SubVibe servers,
no account, no tracking. A typical hour of video costs a few cents, and
results are cached on your device, so rewatching is free.

What it does

• Perfectly synced subtitles — reads the video's own caption track and
  translates ahead of the playhead, so lines appear exactly on time
• Karaoke word highlight — follow along word by word as it's spoken, made
  for language learners
• Dub Mode — natural AI voices speak the translation, with multi-speaker
  voices and adjustable pace
• 90+ target languages, up to two at once (say, English plus your native
  language)
• Style presets and full custom control: font, size, colors, position — drag
  the subtitles anywhere
• Sync dock for time-shifted live streams; recorded videos stay frame-exact
  automatically
• Local library and cache — every translated video is saved on your device;
  replays are instant and free
• Cost meter — see what today's watching actually cost (usually cents)

Where it works

Runs on the players of YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, ZDF, DW, and Udemy,
with more sites on the way. On other pages the extension stays completely
inactive.

Privacy

Your keys and your cache never leave your device. Subtitle text is sent only
to the AI provider you configured, with your own key. No analytics, no
tracking, no middleman. Full policy: see the Privacy link on this page.
